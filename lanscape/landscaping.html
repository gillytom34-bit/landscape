Our Landscaping Services

From initial design to final installation — we offer a complete range of landscaping services to transform your outdoor space.

Landscape Installation & Design

A well-designed landscape enhances your property's curb appeal and usability. Our landscape architects work closely with you to create a customized outdoor space that reflects your style and needs. From flower bed installation to greenscape construction, we handle everything with precision and care, ensuring your landscape remains healthy and visually appealing year-round. Our local landscapers in Watertown are ready to assist with your dream project, no matter how big or small it may be.

Lawn Installation Services

A lush, green lawn is the foundation of a great landscape. We offer professional turf and lawn installation in Watertown tailored to your property's needs.

Sod & Grass Installation — Enjoy an instantly green lawn with high-quality sod that takes root quickly.

Artificial Turf Installation — A low-maintenance, eco-friendly option for a vibrant lawn all year long.

Greenscapes

Greenscape construction focuses on creating sustainable and environmentally friendly landscapes. From native flower plant installations to eco-conscious landscape designs and functional layouts, GreenScape Pros helps homeowners and businesses build green spaces that enhance beauty while promoting biodiversity and reducing water usage.

Irrigation Installation & Repair

A properly functioning irrigation system is essential for maintaining a thriving landscape. We specialize in irrigation installation and repair, ensuring your lawn, flower beds, and greenery receive the right amount of water. We can help you find a solution that works for your property, including highly efficient systems that help conserve water while keeping your outdoor spaces lush and healthy.

Hardscaping

Hardscaping elements add structure, durability, functionality and character to any landscape. Whether you need a charming garden walkway or a sturdy retaining wall, our team expertly installs all sorts of solutions. We specialize in brick and paver walkways, stairs and patios, as well as retaining walls for terracing and to prevent erosion while providing sturdy spaces for plants to grow. For expert hardscaping in Watertown or the surrounding areas, you can rely on us.
