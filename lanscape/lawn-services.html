Lawn Maintenance Services

Keep your lawn lush, green, and healthy year-round with our complete lawn care services — from weekly mowing to seasonal restoration.

Weekly Lawn Mowing

Regular mowing is the foundation of a healthy lawn. Our team provides consistent, professional mowing on a schedule that suits your lawn's growth rate — keeping grass at the ideal height for thick, green growth while preventing weeds and disease. Every visit includes clean, even cuts with sharp blades and full cleanup of clippings.

Edging & Trimming

Sharp, defined edges give your lawn a professionally manicured look. We edge along driveways, sidewalks, flower beds, and fences, then trim around trees, posts, and other obstacles that mowers can't reach. The result: crisp borders that make your entire property look well-maintained.

Fertilization & Weed Control

A healthy lawn starts with the right nutrients. We apply custom fertilization programs tailored to your soil and grass type, plus targeted weed control to eliminate unwanted growth. Our treatments are safe for kids, pets, and the environment — and produce visible, lasting results.

Aeration & Overseeding

Compacted soil prevents air, water, and nutrients from reaching grass roots. Our core aeration service loosens the soil, and we follow up with premium overseeding to fill in thin or bare patches. This powerful combination produces a thicker, greener, more resilient lawn that thrives season after season.

Seasonal Cleanup

Prepare your lawn for every season with our thorough cleanup services. We remove leaves, debris, and dead growth in fall, and refresh beds and turf in spring. Regular seasonal cleanups prevent disease, discourage pests, and set your lawn up for healthy growth all year long.
